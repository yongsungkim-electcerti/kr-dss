Identified effects on cross-border legal interoperability
Has a
Beneficial
interpretation
Justification
:
The regulation explicitly identifies the following applicable policies: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS) as the parent legal basis; Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1502 on assurance levels; Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (eIDAS2); Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR); Regulation (EU) 2018/1725; Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy); Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 on accreditation.
Identified effects on cross-border technical interoperability
Has a
Beneficial
interpretation
Justification
:
The following standards and specifications are identified: ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1 (2025-02) as the primary reference standard against which conformance is assessed; ETSI EN 319 401 as the baseline standard for trust service providers, applicable across multiple clauses (adaptations 1, 3, 4, 5, 6); ENISA "Methodology for sectoral cybersecurity assessments" for FAR/FRR risk analysis in automated identity proofing processes (adaptation 13); NFC passive/active authentication protocols for identity document chip validation (adaptation 11).
