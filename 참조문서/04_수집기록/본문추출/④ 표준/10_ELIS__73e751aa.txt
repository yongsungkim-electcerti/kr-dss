What is the EIRA Library of Interoperability Specifications (ELIS)?
The
EIRA Library of Interoperability Specifications (ELIS)
is an Artifact that
links the
EIRA
architectural building blocks (ABBs) to the standards and specifications
that can be used to implement Solution Building Blocks based on those EIRA ABBs. The aim of this library is to
support solutions architects when modelling using EIRA
.
The latest release of ELIS is the
v7.0.0
.
The
EIRA Library of Interoperability Specifications (ELIS)
is a
CAMSS
Solutions aimed specifically at fostering
interoperability
in the architecture of digital services. EIRA (European Interoperability Reference Architecture) provides a comprehensive set of guidelines and specifications that are essential for developing interoperable systems.
ELIS, as a component of EIRA, serves as a curated library of interoperability specifications
. It supports the implementation of EIRA by
offering a collection of technical standards, interoperability specifications, and best practices designed to guide the development of interoperable digital government services
.
ELIS
ensures that the architectural components and services adhere to common standards, making them capable of seamless communication and data exchange across different IT systems.
The ELIS is also available
through a SPARQL endpoint
(
http://publications.europa.eu/webapi/rdf/sparql
), and you can launch the query and retrieve the data.

For whom? You are a...
Business analyst
to define the different interoperability aspects of European public services.
Solution/Enterprise Architect
in the process of modelling public services using EIRA, as it defines the interoperability aspects of the ABBs contained in it.

Benefits
Alignment with the compliance standards set by the EIF.
Achievement of a threshold guided by criteria that cover all interoperability layers.
Assessment results shown as a convenient score through which to analyze the specification in its different interoperability dimensions.
*
This is a continuous work CAMSS develops as a regular basis for the development of different solutions, however, it could not directly reflect the latest status due to maintenance and enhancement. It is a work in progress to identify different EU legislation and policies regarding the digitalisation of public services and the establishment of standardisation initiatives.
