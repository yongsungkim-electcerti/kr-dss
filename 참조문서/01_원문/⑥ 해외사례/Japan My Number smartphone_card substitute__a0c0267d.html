What is an Electromagnetic Record Substituted for a Card (Certificated function)?

This is issued based on the "Act on the Use of Numbers to Identify a Specific Individual in Administrative Procedures" (Act No. 27 of 2013), and consists of information recorded in the My Number Card of name, address, date of birth, etc. (including former surnames, etc.), and information to prove to the sender that the information is authentic and belongs to the sender. This makes it possible to load the information recorded in the My Number Card onto a smartphone and use the smartphone to perform identity verification.

By storing the information in the smartphone and sending it to the other party, the smartphone alone can prove the identity of the person.

Flow of Procedures (Outline)

card

The destination of each link is the external website "Smartphone-equipped service in function, My Number Card, Digital Agency".

  • *Download and fill out the " Usage Application Registration Form (ZIP / 18 kb) ," which is required for the initial application.
  • 1. Submit initial application and obtain Information Disclosure Application Form
    • Initial Application: forms
  • 2. After filling out the necessary information in the information disclosure application form, apply for information disclosure to obtain the specifications, etc., necessary for development.
    • Disclosure: forms
  • 3. Development is carried out with reference to the specifications. After the development is completed, an application for access to the verification environment is made.
    • *When using the iOS Verifier APIs via the "face-to-face verification SDK" provided by Digital Agency, the procedure in 3 is not required.
    • Verification Access Request: forms
  • 4. Perform a system test in the verification environment, and when it is complete, submit an application to start the service.
    • Getting Started: forms
  • 5. Service commencement upon receipt of written approval from Digital Agency.
