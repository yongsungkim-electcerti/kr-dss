Certified Service Providers Authoritative Trust Registry

The authoritative trust list of services independently certified against the Pan-Canadian Trust Framework (PCTF). Service listed here have passed an independent third-party audit verifying it meets PCTF requirements and completes annual surveillance audits to keep its certification current.

Considering certification? Contact voila@diacc.ca to discuss the benefits.

What Certification Means

  • Independent verification by accredited auditors
  • Compliance with PCTF requirements
  • Annual surveillance audits to maintain certification
